Mathews Boban

I am a second-year Ph.D. student at the Paul G. Allen School of Computer Science and Engineering at the University of Washington, where I am fortunate to be advised by Thomas Rothvoss. Currently, I am working on analysis of mixing time of Markov chains and stochastic localization. Previously, I have been a research assistant at the National University of Singapore hosted by Arnab Bhattacharyya, Michael Choi, and Rahul Jain, and a research assistant at the Technion hosted by Yuval Filmus. I completed my undergraduate degree in Computer Science at IIT Bombay.
